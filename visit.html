Kut Barrage

The Kut Barrage is a dam located in Wasit Governorate in the city of Kut, on the Tigris River. It is considered one of the longest dams in Iraq, with a length of approximately 550 meters and consisting of 56 gates. The project was carried out during the reign of King Faisal I under the supervision of a British company in 1939. The purpose of the project was to control the flow of water from the Tigris River, which was beginning to dry up and threatened the agricultural areas that depend on it for irrigation.
